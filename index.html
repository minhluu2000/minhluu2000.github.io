I'm a PhD student in Computer Engineering at Texas A&M University, working in the Test and Verification Lab. I build machine learning and LLM agent tools that make hardware verification faster, like failure triage, bug generation, and formal debugging. Before this, I was a design verification intern at NXP Semiconductors in Austin, Texas, USA, and then a design verification engineer at Infineon Technologies in Hanoi, Vietnam.
