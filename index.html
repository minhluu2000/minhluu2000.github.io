Minh Luu

Infineon Technologies, Hanoi, Vietnam

I work on design verification at Infineon Technologies. My research uses machine learning and LLM agents to speed up the manual parts of verification: failure triage, bug generation, and formal debugging.

I collaborate with Jiang Hu's group at Texas A&M University.

  1. VCDiag: Classifying Erroneous Waveforms for Failure Triage Acceleration

    Minh Luu, Surya Jasper, Khoi Le, Evan Pan, Michael Quinn, Aakash Tyagi, Jiang Hu

    MLCAD 2025

    Classifies failing simulation waveforms to find the buggy RTL module. Reaches over 94% top-3 accuracy and shrinks raw VCD data by over 120×.

  2. BugGen: A Self-Correcting Multi-Agent LLM Pipeline for Realistic RTL Bug Synthesis

    Surya Jasper, Minh Luu, Evan Pan, Aakash Tyagi, Michael Quinn, Jiang Hu, David Kebo Houngninou

    MLCAD 2025

    Uses LLM agents to create realistic RTL bugs. Produced 500 bugs at 94% functional accuracy and found 104 bugs that OpenTitan regressions missed.
